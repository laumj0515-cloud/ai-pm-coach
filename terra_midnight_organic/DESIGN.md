# Terra — Organic Design (Dark)

## North Star: "Rooted Warmth"
Calm, grounded, and human. Deep nocturnal tones, soft shapes, and natural textures create a warm, approachable experience even in a dark interface.

## Colors
- **Primary (`#6c769b`):** Muted slate blue — actions, navigation, interactive states.
- **Background (`#1b1b1e`):** Deep charcoal — organic and soft, never pure black.
- **Tertiary (`#866e98`):** Dusty lavender — highlights, accents, badges.
- **Palette philosophy:** Earthy and desaturated. No neon or pure-hue colors.
- Use cool-toned neutrals throughout — every gray should have a blue or purple undertone to match the twilight aesthetic.

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Literata — warm serif with personality.
- **Body/Labels:** Nunito Sans — friendly, rounded letterforms.
- Generous line-height (1.6+ for body). Comfortable, unhurried reading.

## Elevation
- Very soft shadows only: `0 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)`.
- Prefer tonal separation over shadows — layer darker and lighter neutral tones to create hierarchy.
- Borders: `outline_variant` at low opacity when needed for definition.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Primary = solid slate blue, rounded corners (8px). Secondary = dark surface + slate text + thin border.
- **Cards:** Deep neutral fill, generous padding (24px), rounded corners (16px). No harsh borders.
- **Inputs:** Darker surface background, rounded, soft slate focus ring.

## Rules
- Large touch targets, generous spacing. Design should feel breathable despite the dark palette.
- Avoid sharp corners and hard contrasts. Everything should feel soft and approachable.
- Images should feel natural and warm — avoid clinical or tech-stock imagery.
